How to read it

Full speed: every machine running non-stop at its clock. That's the demand your factory was built for, and what "missing" is based on.
Now: what the machines did during the game's last 5-minute productivity check.
Fluids are in m³ per minute. Not counted: items in storage, items on trains, trucks and drones, what AWESOME Sinks take, and hand crafting.
Node purity comes from your game files; machine rates come from the game's own recipes.
